Find your gut protocol.

Not everyone's gut is the same. Your diet, region, symptoms, and history determine how your gut will respond to a prebiotic fibre blend. This 3-minute assessment classifies you into one of three Sutra protocols — Gentle Start, Core, or Advanced — or routes you into a 4-week discovery phase if we need more information.
